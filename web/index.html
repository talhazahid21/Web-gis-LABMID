About the study area

Chaman is the headquarters of Chaman District in Balochistan, Pakistan, at about 30.92° N, 66.45° E and roughly 1,338 m above sea level. It lies on the Afghan border next to the Friendship Gate crossing, on the route linking Quetta and Kandahar. The district was created in 2021 by splitting Qila Abdullah District.

This map shows the police stations, parks and petrol stations inside the study extent (dashed rectangle). The points are stored in PostgreSQL/PostGIS and delivered to this page by a REST API.
